# Glossary

Terms the exam uses in a **specific** way, where the everyday meaning will mislead you. Tagged by
topic so you can jump to the module: FSA · QM · CI (Corporate Issuers) · EQ · PM · FI · DER · AI ·
ECON · ETH.

Terms whose precise definition the exam actually tests are marked **⚑**.

---

## A

**Accrual basis** *(FSA)* — Revenue when earned, expense when incurred, regardless of cash. The gap
between accrual earnings and cash flow is the whole of earnings-quality analysis.

**Accrued interest** *(FI)* — Coupon earned by the seller since the last payment date. Added to the
flat price to get the price actually paid.

**Adjusted funds from operations (AFFO)** *(AI)* — FFO minus recurring maintenance capex and leasing
costs. The better REIT proxy for sustainable cash flow.

**Alpha** *(PM)* — Return in excess of what the asset's beta predicted. Jensen's alpha, not "any
outperformance".

**Amortised cost** *(FSA, FI)* — Carrying amount of a bond investment: purchase price adjusted by
the premium or discount amortised to date. Not fair value.

**Annuity due** *(QM)* — Payments at the **beginning** of each period. Worth `(1+r)` times an
ordinary annuity. On the BA II Plus this is the `BGN` mode you must remember to switch back.

**⚑ Arbitrage** *(DER)* — A riskless profit with **zero** net investment. If a strategy requires
capital or bears risk, it is not arbitrage, however attractive.

---

## B

**Backwardation** *(DER, AI)* — Futures price **below** spot. Roll return is **positive**.

**Bank discount yield** *(CI)* — Quoted on **face value** with a **360**-day year. The lowest of the
four money-market yield measures and the least comparable.

**Basis point value (PVBP)** *(FI)* — Money change in a bond's full price for a **one** basis point
yield change. `Money duration × 0.0001`.

**⚑ Beta** *(EQ, PM)* — Sensitivity to the market: `Cov(i,M)/Var(M)`. Measures **systematic** risk
only. Portfolio beta is a weighted average; portfolio standard deviation is not.

**Bond equivalent yield** *(CI, FI)* — Yield restated on a **365**-day, semiannual-compounding basis
so instruments can be compared.

**Book value per share** *(EQ)* — Common equity divided by shares outstanding. A buyback above BVPS
reduces it.

---

## C

**Callable bond** *(FI)* — The **issuer** may redeem early. Caps upside when rates fall, so it
exhibits **negative convexity** and its value is `straight bond − call option`.

**⚑ Capital allocation line vs capital market line** *(PM)* — The CAL joins the risk-free asset to
**any** risky portfolio; the CML is the special case where that portfolio is the **market**.

**Capitalisation rate (cap rate)** *(AI)* — `NOI / Value`. A **lower** cap rate means a **higher**
valuation — the relationship inverts, which is the trap.

**⚑ Carrying amount** *(FSA)* — What the balance sheet says an asset is worth. Not market value, not
cost — cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment.

**Cash conversion cycle** *(FSA, CI)* — `DOH + DSO − Days of payables`. Shorter is better; it can be
negative for firms with strong supplier terms.

**Clean (flat) price** *(FI)* — Quoted price excluding accrued interest. What you see; not what you
pay.

**Coefficient of variation** *(QM)* — `s/x̄`: risk per unit of return. **Lower is better** — the
direction most often reversed under time pressure.

**Commercial paper** *(CI)* — Short-term unsecured corporate borrowing, usually backed by a credit
line.

**Contango** *(DER, AI)* — Futures price **above** spot. Roll return is **negative** — the reason
long-only commodity index returns lag spot.

**⚑ Convexity** *(FI)* — Curvature of the price-yield relationship. Positive convexity means a
price rise for a given yield fall exceeds the price fall for the same yield rise. Always improves
the duration estimate for an option-free bond.

**Cost of trade credit** *(CI)* — The implicit annualised rate of passing up an early-payment
discount. Usually far above the cost of bank debt.

**Covenant** *(FI)* — A contractual constraint on the issuer. **Affirmative** = must do;
**negative** = must not.

**⚑ Credit spread** *(FI)* — Yield over the benchmark, compensating for expected loss plus liquidity
and spread risk. Widens in downturns; the reason credit and equity returns correlate.

---

## D

**Deferred tax asset / liability** *(FSA)* — A timing difference reversing in future periods. A
**DTL** arises when taxable income is **less** than accounting profit — the firm is deferring cash
tax.

**Delta** *(DER)* — Change in option price per unit change in the underlying. Also the hedge ratio.

**Discount margin** *(FI)* — The required spread on a floating-rate note. `DM > quoted margin` →
price below par.

**Dispersion** *(QM)* — Spread around the centre: range, MAD, variance, standard deviation. Never
the centre itself.

**⚑ Duration gap** *(FI)* — `Macaulay duration − investment horizon`. **Positive** → price risk
dominates, so rising rates hurt. **Negative** → reinvestment risk dominates, so falling rates hurt.
Zero → immunised.

**DuPont analysis** *(FSA)* — Decomposition of ROE into margin, turnover and leverage. Tells you
*why* ROE changed, which is what the question asks.

---

## E

**⚑ Effective annual rate** *(QM, FI)* — The rate accounting for compounding within the year. The
only rate at which two differently-compounded instruments can be compared.

**Effective duration** *(FI)* — Sensitivity to a shift in the **benchmark curve**, measured by
repricing. The **only** duration valid for bonds with embedded options.

**Efficient frontier** *(PM)* — Portfolios with the highest return for each level of risk. Its
leftmost point is the global minimum-variance portfolio.

**Enterprise value** *(EQ)* — Value of the whole operating business: market cap plus debt, preferred
and minorities, **less cash**. Pairs only with pre-interest flows.

**⚑ Exercise (intrinsic) value** *(DER)* — What an option is worth if exercised now: `max(0, S−X)`
for a call. Total option value = exercise value + time value, and time value is never negative
before expiry.

---

## F

**Fair value hierarchy** *(FSA)* — Level 1 quoted prices · Level 2 observable inputs · Level 3
unobservable. Level 3 is where earnings-quality concerns concentrate.

**⚑ FCFF vs FCFE** *(FSA, EQ)* — FCFF is pre-debt and discounts at **WACC** to give enterprise
value; FCFE is post-debt and discounts at the **cost of equity** to give equity value. Mismatching
them is the single most common valuation error.

**Fisher effect** *(ECON, QM)* — Nominal ≈ real + expected inflation. The multiplicative form is
exact; the additive form is the approximation.

**Float-adjusted market cap** *(EQ)* — Market cap using only shares available to public investors.
The standard index weighting.

**Forward rate** *(QM, FI)* — Rate for a future period implied by today's spot curve. With an
upward-sloping curve, **forward > spot > par**.

**Funds from operations (FFO)** *(AI)* — Net income plus depreciation less gains on property sales.
The REIT earnings measure.

---

## G

**Geometric mean return** *(QM)* — The compound rate that links start to end. Always **≤**
arithmetic mean; the gap grows with volatility. Use it for past performance, the arithmetic mean for
a single-period expectation.

**Gordon growth model** *(QM, EQ)* — `V0 = D1/(r−g)`. Requires `g < r` and constant growth forever.
Extremely sensitive to `r − g`.

---

## H

**H-model** *(EQ)* — Two-stage DDM with growth declining **linearly**. `H` is **half** the length of
the decline period, not the whole of it.

**⚑ Herfindahl-Hirschman Index** *(ECON)* — Sum of squared market shares **in percentage points**.
Below 1,500 unconcentrated; above 2,500 highly concentrated. Squaring decimals is the trap.

**High water mark** *(AI)* — The peak NAV on which performance fees have already been paid. Prevents
charging twice for recovering the same gains.

**Hurdle rate** *(AI)* — The return that must be cleared before performance fees accrue. **Hard**:
fee on the excess only. **Soft**: fee on the whole return once cleared.

---

## I

**Immunisation** *(FI)* — Matching duration to the horizon so price and reinvestment risk offset.

**Impairment** *(FSA)* — Writing an asset down to recoverable amount. IFRS allows reversal; US GAAP
does **not** for assets held for use.

**Investment policy statement** *(PM)* — The governing document: objectives (return, risk) and
constraints (liquidity, horizon, tax, legal, unique).

---

## J

**Justified P/E** *(EQ)* — The multiple implied by fundamentals — `payout/(r−g)` leading. Compare it
to the actual multiple to judge mispricing.

---

## K

**Key rate duration** *(FI)* — Sensitivity to a change at **one** maturity with the rest of the
curve unchanged. Key rate durations sum to effective duration.

**⚑ Kurtosis** *(QM)* — Tail weight. **Excess** kurtosis is kurtosis minus 3; positive excess
(leptokurtic) means fatter tails and understated tail risk if you assume normality.

---

## L

**Leptokurtic** *(QM)* — Fat-tailed. Typical of financial returns; the reason normal-based VaR
understates extreme losses.

**⚑ LIFO reserve** *(FSA)* — `FIFO inventory − LIFO inventory`. Add it to LIFO inventory for a FIFO
balance sheet; subtract the **change** from LIFO COGS for FIFO COGS.

**LIFO liquidation** *(FSA)* — Selling from old, low-cost layers when inventory quantities fall.
Inflates margins unsustainably; detect it by falling LIFO reserve **and** falling inventory
quantity.

**Loss given default** *(FI)* — The fraction of exposure not recovered. `1 − recovery rate`.

---

## M

**⚑ Macaulay duration** *(FI)* — The weighted average time to receipt of a bond's cash flows, in
years. Equals maturity for a zero-coupon bond. Divide by `(1+r)` for modified duration.

**Margin of safety** — *Not a CFA Level I term.* Do not use it in place of the curriculum's language.

**⚑ Material non-public information** *(ETH)* — Information likely to affect price that has not been
broadly disseminated. The remedy is always to **abstain** — Standard II(A). Disclosure to the client
does not cure it; selective disclosure compounds it.

**Modified duration** *(FI)* — The percentage price change per 1% yield change:
`%ΔP ≈ −ModDur × ΔY`.

**Money market yield** *(CI)* — Holding-period yield annualised on a 360-day basis. Sits between
bank discount yield and bond equivalent yield.

**Multiplier effect** *(ECON)* — The amplification of a fiscal change through the spending chain.
`1/[1 − MPC(1−t)]`.

---

## N

**Negative convexity** *(FI)* — Price appreciation decelerating as yields fall, caused by a call or
prepayment option. Callable bonds and MBS exhibit it.

**Net operating income** *(AI)* — Property revenue less operating expenses, **before** financing,
tax and depreciation. The numerator of the cap rate.

**Nominal spread** *(FI)* — Yield spread over a single benchmark yield. Ignores the shape of the
curve, which is why Z-spread exists.

---

## O

**⚑ Option-adjusted spread** *(FI)* — Z-spread less the value of the embedded option. For a callable
bond, `OAS < Z-spread`. OAS is the comparable measure across bonds with different option features.

**Ordinary annuity** *(QM)* — Payments at period **end**. The calculator's default; also the default
assumption in exam questions unless stated otherwise.

---

## P

**Par curve** *(FI)* — Yields at which bonds of each maturity would trade at par. Bootstrapped into
spot rates.

**Passive vs active** *(PM)* — Passive tracks a benchmark; active seeks alpha. The distinction is
intent, not outcome.

**Payout ratio** *(EQ)* — Dividends divided by earnings. `1 − retention ratio`.

**Prepayment risk** *(FI)* — Uncertainty in MBS cash flow timing. Falling rates → **contraction**
risk. Rising rates → **extension** risk.

**⚑ Probability of default** *(FI)* — Likelihood of failing to pay as promised.
`Expected loss = PD × LGD × Exposure`.

**Profitability index** *(CI)* — `PV of future cash flows / Initial investment`. Equals `1 + NPV/I`,
so PI > 1 exactly when NPV > 0.

**Putable bond** *(FI)* — The **holder** may sell it back. Value is `straight bond + put option`, so
its price floor is higher and its duration lower than an otherwise identical straight bond.

---

## Q

**Quoted margin** *(FI)* — The fixed spread over the reference rate on a floating-rate note.
Compare it to the discount margin to price the FRN.

---

## R

**Realised vs unrealised** *(AI)* — DPI is realised (cash distributed); RVPI is unrealised (NAV
still held). TVPI is their sum, and a high TVPI made of RVPI is a claim, not a result.

**Reinvestment risk** *(FI)* — The risk that coupons are reinvested at lower rates. Dominates when
the horizon exceeds Macaulay duration.

**Retention ratio** *(EQ)* — `b = 1 − payout`. Multiply by ROE for sustainable growth.

**⚑ Return on invested capital** *(CI)* — `EBIT(1−t)/Average invested capital`. Value is created
when ROIC exceeds WACC; growth without that spread destroys value.

**Roll return** *(AI, DER)* — The return from rolling a futures position forward. **Negative** in
contango, **positive** in backwardation.

---

## S

**⚑ Security market line vs capital market line** *(PM)* — The SML prices **systematic** risk (beta)
and holds for **any** asset. The CML prices **total** risk (σ) and holds only for efficient
portfolios. Choosing the wrong axis is the classic PM error.

**Semi-deviation** *(QM)* — Dispersion of observations **below** a target only. Denominator is still
`n−1` over the whole sample, not the subset count.

**⚑ Sharpe vs Treynor** *(PM)* — Sharpe divides excess return by **total** risk (σ) and judges a
whole portfolio; Treynor divides by **beta** and judges a component of a diversified portfolio.

**Spot rate** *(FI, QM)* — The zero-coupon yield for a single future date. Discount each cash flow
at its own spot rate for an arbitrage-free price.

**Sustainable growth rate** *(EQ)* — `b × ROE`: growth financeable from retained earnings without
changing leverage.

---

## T

**Time value (of an option)** *(DER)* — Option value less exercise value. Decays to zero at expiry.

**Total value to paid-in (TVPI)** *(AI)* — `DPI + RVPI`. The headline private-equity multiple.

**⚑ Treasury stock method** *(FSA)* — For diluted EPS: assume options are exercised and the proceeds
buy back shares at the **average** market price. Net new shares are the dilution.

---

## U

**Unearned (deferred) revenue** *(FSA)* — Cash received before delivery. A **liability**; its
increase adds to cash from customers.

**Utility function** *(PM)* — `U = E(R) − ½Aσ²`. Higher `A` = more risk-averse = steeper indifference
curves.

---

## V

**Value in use** *(FSA)* — PV of future cash flows from continued use. Under IFRS, recoverable amount
is the higher of value in use and fair value less costs to sell.

**Vega** *(DER)* — Sensitivity to volatility. Positive for **both** calls and puts — the one Greek
where calls and puts share a sign.

---

## W

**⚑ Weighted average cost of capital** *(CI)* — `wd·rd(1−t) + wp·rp + we·re` at **market** weights.
Only the debt component is tax-adjusted.

**Working capital** *(FSA, CI)* — Current assets less current liabilities. In cash flow analysis,
asset increases are **uses** of cash and liability increases are **sources**.

---

## Y

**Yield to worst** *(FI)* — The lowest of YTM and every possible yield-to-call. The conservative
yield for a callable bond.

---

## Z

**⚑ Z-spread** *(FI)* — The constant spread added to **every** spot rate that makes the discounted
cash flows equal the market price. Reflects the whole curve; ignores optionality, which is what OAS
corrects.

**Z-score** *(QM)* — `(X − μ)/σ`: observations from the mean, in standard deviations. The input to
every normal-distribution probability question.
