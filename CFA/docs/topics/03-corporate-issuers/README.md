## Why this topic sits here

Corporate Issuers is studied **third**, immediately after FSA and the first QM block, and that
ordering is load-bearing.

This topic asks you to **use** what you have just learned. The cash conversion cycle in LM4 is built
from the activity ratios in FSA LM11. The NPV and IRR work in LM5 is the discounting from QM LM4
applied to projects. The WACC in LM6 requires the cost of equity, which CAPM supplies. Without those
foundations this topic is a set of disconnected formulas; with them, it is one continuous argument
about how a company should deploy capital.

It also feeds forward. **WACC is the discount rate in Equity LM6.** **ROIC versus WACC is the
central test of value creation** in company analysis (Equity LM10). And the credit-analysis
perspective in LM2 — the structural conflict between lenders and shareholders — is what Fixed
Income LM14–LM16 build on.

At 6–9% the weight is modest, but the concepts are used in three other topics. This is the best
return-per-hour block in the plan.

## The formulas this topic lives on

Four things from this topic must be automatic.

```
1. CCC = DOH + DSO − Days of payables          — negative is a structural advantage
2. NPV and IRR on the BA II Plus CF worksheet  — under 60 seconds for a 5-flow project
3. ROIC = EBIT(1−t) / Average invested capital — value created only when ROIC > WACC
4. WACC = wd·rd(1−t) + wp·rp + we·re           — MARKET weights; only debt is tax-adjusted
```

And two rules that decide questions on their own:
- **Sunk costs excluded, opportunity costs included** (LM5)
- **When NPV and IRR conflict on mutually exclusive projects, NPV wins** (LM5)
