> **Never cut the Ethics hour.** It is the largest topic on the exam (15–20%), the designated
> tie-breaker for borderline candidates, and the one that responds least to cramming. If a week
> goes badly, take the time out of the lowest-weight core topic ahead of you — never out of Ethics.
