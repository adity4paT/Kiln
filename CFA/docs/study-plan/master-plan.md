## Weekly rhythm

| Day of week | Addition to the daily loop |
| --- | --- |
| Mon–Thu | Standard: 4h core + 1h Ethics |
| Fri | Standard + **formula drill** (cover the right column of `resources/formula-sheet.md`) |
| Sat | Standard + **weekly mixed set** — 30–60 questions drawn from *everything covered so far*, not just this week |
| Sun | **Lighter day.** 2–3h: clear `weak-areas.md` entries, Ethics hour, plan the week ahead |

Sunday being lighter is load-bearing, not a reward. A plan with no slack has no way to absorb a bad
week, and a plan that never absorbs a bad week gets abandoned in month 3.

## If your timeline isn't 150 days

| Available | What to change |
| --- | --- |
| **~200 days** | Keep the same order and hour allocations, drop to 4h/day (3 core + 1 Ethics). Add a dedicated 6-week revision phase at the end. **This is the most comfortable version of this plan.** |
| **~150 days** | Run it as written. Revision must be carved from the plan — see `revision-phase.md`. |
| **~100 days** | 7–8h/day, same order. Cut the Sunday light day to a half day. Realistic only if you are not working full time. |
| **< 90 days** | Do not attempt all 102 modules at depth. Triage by weight: full depth on Ethics, FSA, Equity, Fixed Income, Portfolio Management; LOS-and-formulas-only on Derivatives, Economics, and Alternatives. |

## Honest caveat about the 150 days

The 150 days cover **first-pass coverage of all 102 modules**. Retrieval (Pass 2) and the weekly
mixed sets are built into the four core hours, but a full revision-and-mocks phase is *not* inside
the 150. If your exam is exactly at day 150, you must either:

- **start earlier** (the 200-day variant above — strongly preferred), or
- **compress the low-weight blocks** — take 3 days from Derivatives, 3 from Alternatives, and 2
  from Economics to buy 8 revision days before the exam, accepting a shallower first pass on
  13–27 questions' worth of material.

The second option is a real trade, not a free one. Make it deliberately, and make it in month 1 —
not in week 20 when the choice makes itself.
